How it works

The whole idea in five sentences. This is the tour you'd show anyone — no crypto knowledge needed.

Design mockup · sample data

You set the rules

How much your agent may spend — per request and per month — and who it may pay.

Your agent works

Every payment it attempts hits the gate on-chain first. The gate checks it against your rules.

Inside the rules — automatic

The payment goes through. You are not bothered. It's logged in History.

Over the rules — it stops

Nothing moves. The request lands in Waiting for you, with a plain explanation of why.

You decide — by signature

Approve once, refuse, or change the rules. Every option requires your wallet signature. Every step is recorded on-chain, forever.
